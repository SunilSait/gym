HOW IT ALL STARTED

IronPeak began in 2012 when founder Marcus Reid noticed something missing in his neighborhood: a truly accessible, high-quality fitness center where anyone — from first-timers to competitive athletes — could feel at home.

With just 2,000 sq ft, a handful of weights, and an unshakeable belief in the power of community fitness, Marcus opened the doors to what would become the neighborhood's most beloved gym.

Today, IronPeak spans 12,000 sq ft with state-of-the-art equipment, 18 certified trainers, and over 2,400 members who call it their second home.
